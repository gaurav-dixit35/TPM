# Q2 — Prototype guide

Flow: Front Desk admits → Nurse records temperature → Doctor reviews and visits → Doctor records a verdict → Front Desk processes an eligible discharge → Centre Lead reviews aggregate outcomes. On the demo sign-in screen, choose Nurse, Doctor, Front Desk, or Centre Lead, then enter that stakeholder view. The role menu can preview the other views; this is a demo control, not authentication. Nurse selects Record, enters °C, and saves. Doctor sees measured patients as ready; an unmeasured patient is explicitly waiting on Nurse. A discharge recommendation is available only after the configured three-day fever-free criterion. Front Desk processes the handoff and frees a bed. Patient names open their profile view. Data is fictional and local to the browser. Reviewer screenshots should be captured at desktop and 360px mobile widths.
