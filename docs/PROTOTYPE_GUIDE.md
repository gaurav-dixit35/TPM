# Q2 — Prototype guide

Flow: Front Desk admits → Nurse records temperature → Doctor reviews and visits → Doctor records a verdict → Front Desk processes an eligible discharge → Centre Lead reviews aggregate outcomes. On demo sign-in, choose Nurse, Doctor, Front Desk, or Centre Lead. The Centre Lead opens on Dashboard, which summarizes occupancy, temperature and visit coverage, role queues, fever flags, a seven-day activity trend, and caveated outcomes. Dashboard work rows link to their role queues. Other users can open Dashboard from the main navigation. The role menu previews other views; it is not authentication. Nurse selects Record, enters °C, and saves. Doctor sees measured patients as ready; an unmeasured patient is explicitly waiting on Nurse. A discharge recommendation is available only after the configured three-day fever-free criterion. Front Desk processes the handoff and frees a bed. Patient names open their profile view. Data is fictional and local to the browser. Reviewer screenshots should be captured at desktop and 360px mobile widths.
