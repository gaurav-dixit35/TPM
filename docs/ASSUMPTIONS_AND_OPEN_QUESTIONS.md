# Assumptions and open questions

- 38.0°C is a visual prototype fever threshold; obtain clinician-approved threshold and measurement protocol.
- “Three full days” is approximated with three consecutive qualifying calendar-day readings; confirm whether elapsed 72 hours or another rule applies.
- Missing readings break the streak; confirm policy.
- One official reading/day is shown; determine repeat-reading and correction workflow with audit history.
- Suspect threshold, plausibility range, correction window and overdue time are demo assumptions.
- Mortality metric uses deaths divided by closed cases; confirm cohort, period, transfer handling, and whether success means survival or cure.
- Discharge recommendation is gated on the three-day criterion; confirm who is authorized to sign off and what discharge documentation is required.
- Demo sign-in and role switching/localStorage are not access control; data is not shared across devices.
- Product is an assessment prototype only; no real patient data or care decisions.
